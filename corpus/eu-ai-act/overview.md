# EU AI Act — curated reference notes for Aegis demos
# Source framing: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act). Not legal advice.

## Risk taxonomy (simplified)
- Prohibited (Art. 5): practices banned in the Union
- High-risk (Art. 6 + Annex III): conformity assessment + Art. 9–15 obligations
- Limited / transparency (Art. 50): disclosure for chatbots, synthetic content, etc.
- Minimal: residual systems with voluntary codes of conduct

## Public-sector hotspot (NL municipalities)
Municipal citizen-service chatbots often combine:
1. Limited-risk transparency (user must know they talk to AI)
2. Possible high-risk adjacency when the bot influences access to essential public services
3. Strong expectations for human escalation, logging, and Dutch-language clarity

## GPAI / foundation models
General-purpose AI models have additional provider obligations (documentation,
copyright policy, systemic-risk evaluations for the most capable models).
Aegis focuses on *system* conformity assessment drafts for deployers/providers
of AI systems in public-sector contexts.
